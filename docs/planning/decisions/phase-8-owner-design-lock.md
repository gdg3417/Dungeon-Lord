## 2. Current implementation boundary after merged PR #245

**Implemented at the reviewed baseline:** one automatic foreground, one-party, **single Active Floor 1** production timed-C2 progression loop under a distinct profile, with actual configured admission, committed event advancement, supported active-run editing, atomic C2 consequence/monster reconciliation, persistent history, vacant-floor *existing* monster recovery and a new party observing committed layout changes. The session-local compatible just-completed run can be replayed; reopened historical runs retain summary-only behavior. Timed B1 and C0/C2 compatible checkpoint contracts remain distinct; current canonical save schema is **15**. Owner and Windows/editor qualification evidence is in `Docs/testing/evidence/living-dungeon-b3b2/qualification.md` (PR #245).

**Not thereby implemented:** automatic simultaneous multi-floor runs/descent queues; full eligible **offline adventurer** catch-up; Phase 8 monster roster/classes/boss skills; dual paid research tracks; ranked five-floor permit; per-node paid stock and partial refunds; Ward; Pillar/Flooded environmental simulation; paid trap reset/recharge/replacement; final player-facing systems; native low-end mobile or full five-floor throughput. Current #245 observed **synchronous event stalls** of roughly 236–424 ms in the measured production path even after profiling corrections. Treat them as a later measured performance risk, **not** an unverified release blocker or a 30 FPS pass claim.

The prototype `PlayerResearchActionHandler` exposes `CanClaimLocalMvp`, but current code remains **single-slot** and its visible flags state `WouldChargeCosts=false`, `WouldProcessOfflineProgress=false`, `CanClaimProduction=false`. D196 approves future MVP behavior; it is **not evidence those complete semantics ship now**. Present-day floor construction still consumes Floor 2's `ac_100` permission and authored `spatial.floor.02`; canonical Floor 1/2 source records only are currently present.

## 3. Floor ranks, progression, capacity and environment — D01–D08, D174–D181

- The MVP has **one main dungeon and five floors maximum**. Additional floor access is a **unified, ranked Architecture research permission covering Floors 1–5**; the pre-existing standalone `ac_100` Floor 2 entitlement is a legacy compatibility input, **not** a second simultaneously chargeable path after conversion (**D174 supersedes D46**). Each new permitted rank requires its own authored timed project and applicable upfront research cost/claim. A completed rank grants *permission*, not a floor, free construction, activation or automatic mana expense.
- Floor construction and activation remain separate explicit player actions governed by physical bounds, graph validity, capacity, funds and existing contiguous Active-prefix rules. Floors 3–5 require authored records, not a calculation by hardcoded index. Floor 5 should represent a **noticeably larger authored spatial milestone**, but neither a magic numeric threshold nor compulsory boss room is authorized. A boss can legally appear on **any eligible floor/room** (D175–D176). The historical spatial design suggests Floor 3 ~3–4 rooms and 2–3 corridors; Floor 4 ~4–5 rooms and 3–4 corridors; Floor 5 ~5–6 rooms and ~7–9 combined buildable route pieces, **composition targets only**. Exact sizes/capacities are future reviewed data; Floor 1 is 12×12/capacity 60 and Floor 2 is 14×14/capacity 80 in existing authoring.
- Players define dungeon identity through build composition and meaningful layouts, not automatic per-floor element themes or forced bosses. Existing same-floor required route and optional detour, no generalized maze/loop, and authored room/corridor capacities remain the initial MVP rule unless separately amended.
- Two **separately researched** Architecture environmental room definitions are approved: **Pillar Chamber** (solid pillar cells block **movement AND line of sight** for ranged attacks) and **Flooded Basic Room** (authored shallow-water traversable cells delay **logical movement time**). Opacity and nontraversability are **distinct data/semantics**, not automatically inferred from all reserved tiles. Water does not change animation speed instead of simulation time. No freeform terrain painting, lava hazard or rubble system is approved by this packet.
- Every new room is authored in the canonical production package with legal tiles, sockets, passability/opacity/timing effects, capacity and localization. Verify line-of-sight denials, deterministic paths, offline exact-time effects, invalid placements, snapshot isolation and player comprehension.

## 4. Research product rules — D13, D45–D48, D96–D109, D137–D140, D196

- Research presents category tabs and an understandable expandable/clustered tree. **General Research and Blueprint Research have independent one-project-at-a-time slots**; neither borrows the other slot and there is no auto-queue or cancel in MVP. D106–D109 **supersede D47 and historical one-global-slot texts**.
- Every chosen eligible project is manually started. At **start**, validate correct track, stable node/version/prerequisites, usable unprotected mana and durable predecessor, then **deduct configured mana exactly once and atomically** with active-project state. Do not reserve research mana throughout the timer, pause an already-paid timer for low later funds or silently waive cost. D138 supersedes earlier research reserve descriptions.
- Projects progress under approved local elapsed-time policy, **including eligible offline time**, with explicit start/progress/ready/claim/completed distinctions. Claim occurs **once**, with no early unlock or free-slot reuse before committed completion. MVP starts/claims may be locally validated without internet (**D196**), but do not portray this as tamper-proof network certification. Before **initial public production release**, require actual online backend verification for research start and claim, retain safe pending state and test offline/reconnect/replay behavior. Do not invent a server/endpoint for the documentation PR.
- General Research owns Monsterology, Architecture, Trapcraft and relevant systems; Blueprint Research owns **individually researched item/material-tier blueprints** and still consumes General permissions where authored. Research choices and count-gated class prerequisites are additive and nonexclusive. Tier-guided, authored project times and costs, disclosure/reveal, reason codes and localization require content validation; exact numbers and a second writable time authority are not approved.
- Historic **one-global-slot** MVP checklist rows and specs remain historical, **superseded for future Phase 8 MVP**. Current runtime is still one slot: changing it requires reviewed save and migration/reopen behavior.

## 5. Monsterology progression, roster and optional bosses — D09–D44, D144–D173

- **Family** (Goblinoid/Undead), **form/species**, **individually researched class**, and **individual level** are distinct. Researching a class grants permission, not an automatically upgraded instance; advancing/recruiting still pays its appropriate configured per-monster mana/obligations. Form advancement must never grant an unresearched named class. Previous researched/owned class investment becomes dormant where no longer compatible; one active class per monster, paid respecialization when authorized. Preserve stable instance ID, level and proportional injury on a valid evolution with deterministic rounding. Evolved and directly recruited equivalent configurations have equivalent capabilities, and family branches are **nonexclusive** (D158-CL1).
- MVP Goblinoid starts **Goblin** with individually researched **Archer, Spearman, Rogue, Shaman**. Goblin Warrior is **excluded as a separate new MVP class**, but old stable `mon_300`/legacy save/research mappings must be examined, not erased or falsely counted. Goblin Shaman requires ordinary family permission rather than an obsolete Goblin Warrior/Archer dependency. **Hobgoblin** and **Bugbear** forms each require **any two distinct researched basic Goblin classes**. Their initially selected MVP class paths are **Hobgoblin Tactician** and **Bugbear Crusher**. An eligible Hobgoblin class is required for **Hobgoblin Warlord** research alongside genuine coordination achievement evidence.
- MVP Undead starts **Skeleton** (independently researched **Warrior** and **Archer** classes). **Zombie** has independently researched **Ravager** and **Bulwark** classes. **Wight** form needs **both distinct basic Skeleton class researches**, then has researched **Sentinel** and **Reaper** paths. **Ghoul** form needs **any two distinct researched Zombie classes** (currently Ravager/Bulwark) and has its approved distinctive base but **no second MVP class**. **Wight Lord** research needs any **one researched Wight class** plus authentic wounded-resilience mitigation achievement evidence. D166–D173 supersede the older tentative and exact-class-specific gate proposals.
- The two chosen **MVP boss branches** are Hobgoblin Warlord and Wight Lord, with each family having one permitted boss set maximum and a distinctive signature boss. Bosses are **optional**, not mandatory Floor 5 or terminal encounters. Each eligible placement consumes its ordinary configured room/content capacity and cost; initial MVP boss footprint remains standard one tile with authored suitable-room restrictions, not universal huge multi-tile demand. High mana/reserve pressure is intended, exact numbers not locked.
- Baseline family identity: Goblinoid **class-aware tactical target selection/coordination**, Undead **bounded wounded resilience and attrition**. An injured Wight's configured damage mitigation actually reduces eligible incoming damage, not an invented generic immunity. All balance/defense/damage effects live in reviewed config, not code constants.
- Legacy `Monsterology_Spec_v1` calls champions/elite evolutions post-MVP and describes an obsolete roster. Those clauses are superseded **only where the above owner-approved Phase 8 roster/class/boss scope directly conflicts**. Research/content IDs, save carryover and authoring schema require explicit technical review.

## 6. Combat abilities and achievement event evidence — D147–D156, D182–D185, D198, D201

- **Crossbowman Precision Shot:** legal stronger single-target ranged action; **Boneguard Shield Brace:** gives up its usual attack for temporary defensive protection; **Warlord Rallying Order:** temporary buff to **ALL eligible same-room Goblinoid allies**; **Wight Lord Dread Presence:** multi-target debuff to eligible adventurers. Each uses the normal action economy, requires the respective researched/unlocked class/form and no separate ability-only research. Advanced nonboss abilities: **once per encounter**. Boss signatures: reusable at configured **cooldown** under normal action constraints. Exact targets/effects/expiration/cooldowns require authored contract.
- **No stacking of multiple same-named signature effects on a recipient**: use the strongest eligible instance, with deterministic canonical tie-breaking; different named effects may coexist under their approved types (**D198**). Legal multiple boss placements are not banned simply to prevent stacking.
- **Crossbowman gate (D182):** count only completed, legal **Goblin Archer ranged attacks that deal positive HP damage**, pooled over eligible Archers. Attempts/misses or zero real HP damage do not count.
- **Boneguard gate (D183):** count actual HP damage a **Skeleton Warrior survives from each individual incoming attack**; the fatal attack itself adds no credit. Previously credited survived-hit damage is not erased if the Warrior later dies. Pool across eligible Warriors.
- **Warlord gate (D184 + D201):** at least one eligible Hobgoblin and **another distinct eligible Goblinoid** must each complete legal attacks against **the same adventurer in the same encounter**; attacks need not deal positive HP damage. **One credit maximum per unique qualifying encounter**, regardless of repeated qualifying pairs. Do not hardcode Tactician as the only eligible Hobgoblin class.
- **Wight Lord gate (D185):** actual positive damage **prevented by low-HP Wight wounded resilience**, pooling qualifying Sentinel and Reaper evidence; mere survival, theoretical prevention or boss-only evidence does not count. Progress must be achievable *before* boss research.
- Persist authentic canonical combat action and mitigation receipts, with versioned reason and encounter identities, atomic achievement increment, dedupe across resume/offline and no replay-UI-authoritative side effects. Thresholds are authored/unselected; illustrative 50/100 candidates are not approvals. **D150** requires **full valid offline interval exact event credit** for eligible objectives; no time-rate estimates or fabricated hits/encounters.

## 7. Loot research, catalog, stock, extraction and partial refund — D51–D55, D89–D143, D189–D197

- Starter loot is bounded and family-themed; nonstarter items require authentic eligible adventurer-origin discovery, analysis samples and **one individual Blueprint project per item/material tier**. A first valid discovery is also the first sample; sample counts persist through analysis and clear only their redundant counts after valid Blueprint completion. At most one sample per item+tier per party visit; nonlethal sources at every tier must be possible, but no mandatory pity timer or guaranteed drop is approved. Research unlocks affect **future party visit snapshots**, never silently recalculate an in-progress party. Blueprint availability depends on approved General/Arcanology permissions.
- Approved MVP loot breadth includes **Sword, Daggers, Spear, Bow, Staff**, each with Bronze/Iron/Steel quality tiers; **Light and Heavy Armor** tier variants; **Protective Spell Scroll, Magical Charm, Basic Rations (starter), Healing Potion (researched)**; themed material/relic/trade goods including nonstarter Iron Ore and Bronze Relic. No stand-alone ammunition item or in-combat Healing Potion consumption for MVP. The Staff tier denotes capability/quality rather than literal metal composition. Exact complete catalog, variants, rarity and item IDs are **authoring gates**—the register contains candidate counts, not a committed production dataset.
- Items/blueprints have authored allowed rarity/capability distributions, authentic discovery sources and class affinities. Each ordinary reusable **loot-node instance**, not each room, has a persisted weighted focus (including Mixed), with focus-specific authored eligibility/weights. If preferred focus has no **affordable legal candidate**, temporarily fall back to approved Mixed eligibility **without changing the saved focus**. Basic/Hidden Cache/Glittering Hoard use independently authored reward profiles under one item and research authority. Expected **extracted world value** remains an attraction/Heat/economic input, not a second mana source.
- **Paid stock authority (D130/D131/D189–D194):** when a real reward item is **created/stocked** for a node, debit its actual configured cost **once**, excluding unaffordable outcomes *before deterministic selection*. An ignored item **remains stocked** and incurs no repeated fee. Actual collection removes it from that node; when its **floor becomes vacant**, eligible depleted nodes attempt automatic affordable refill, only after **eligible monster recovery and authorized paid trap restoration**. Canonical deterministic floor/room/assignment ordering governs scarce funds; unaffordable nodes remain empty and retry at later eligible **vacant-floor** opportunities. No mid-party refill, speculative reward minting, duplicated wallet or free reroll. A node shows selected **stocked / empty / waiting for mana** status; a mana-starved halo must be supplemented by accessible icon/shape/text.
- **D195 + D197 narrow exception:** an item actually collected but **not successfully extracted** by its collecting party grants **a configured partial fraction of the item's ACTUAL ORIGINAL PAID CREATION MANA COST**, exactly once at authoritative eligible settlement. Preserve existing survivor-based extraction economics through a **lossless deterministic item or portion attribution**; mixed-survivor outcomes must not count the same portion both extracted and refunded. Ignored/uncollected and fully extracted portions have **no refund**. The refund never rematerializes the original item or changes a node that has since been refilled. No current market/world-value sale conversion, grant from an unpaid item, unlimited credit or arbitrary new loot-earning loop. The refund fraction, rounding, receipts, temporal ordering and capacity/protected-reserve interaction need versioned technical specification and approval before code.
- MVP player presentation uses **minimal popups only during observed live loot events**, **not** a mandatory per-run item ledger. Player inventory is not a second authoritative physical item-owner system; item instance/provenance required for deterministic stock/refund is an internal game economy authority.

## 8. Trapcraft: Treasure Ward and three restoration modes — D49–D50, D59–D88, D186–D188, D199–D200

- The **one selected new MVP trap archetype** is **Treasure Ward**, which protects **one chosen room loot-node instance**. It shares that loot node's physical tile as a **strictly scoped co-location exception**, but occupies its *own trap-category capacity* separately from the loot node's *loot-category capacity*. A room node may have **one active Ward**, and a Ward links to one legal node. No general room-content overlap, corridor Ward in MVP or new second trap system is implied.
- A Ward uses separately researched Trapcraft security permissions and paid configured per-instance upgrades, with a player-selectable legal security difficulty defaulting to the lowest unlocked. Each eligible party receives **one bypass attempt per linked node per visit**, triggered **on legitimate attempted collection**, not room entry; a different party can attempt the same still-linked Ward later without automatic recharge cost. Resolve a seeded, bounded success probability from authored magical-seal aptitudes (primary Mage/Cleric contribution plus reduced other eligible team support; parties with no specialist still attempt), relative level/security and canonical identity. Every legal matchup retains a chance of both success and failure. Failed bypass damages the **primary eligible magical specialist**, or **the attempted collector if no eligible specialist exists** (D73), blocks **only linked loot**, and does not forcibly end the visit. Do not invent check values or targeting tie-breaks.
- Ward and loot have independent stable instances/custody. Moving a protected node keeps legal attachment; removing/unassigning a Ward alone returns it to existing custody without removing stock; an unassigned owned Ward can be freely relinked to another eligible unprotected node. Linked node retirement/unassignment applies the separately approved custody/association policy, with no orphan or duplication. Changes after party start do not rewrite its captured Ward rules. Any edit must atomically validate spatial occupancy, separate capacities, financial investment, association and return-to-custody status. See D79–D86 and later D186/D187 for effective cases.
- **Separate general trap restoration (D199 + D200):** authored trap definitions select **single-use paid replacement**, **mechanical paid reset** (Snare Trap example), or **magical paid recharge** (Chilling Sigil example). Replacement may require a new instance/ownership contract; reset and recharge preserve the apparatus's stable identity and paid investment where valid. Each action uses its **trap-specific configured mana cost**; no universal free mechanic, hardcoded rate or maintenance NPC. Spent/unready traps do not trigger, especially in the same party visit. Automatic legal restoration occurs on a safe vacant-floor opportunity **after monsters, before loot**, subject to available canonical funds and exact-once persistence. D200 does **not** impose a new mana fee on every Ward bypass attempt; Ward is governed by its separate per-party reuse rule.
- **Do not classify Spike Trap as single-use by its name.** Full per-definition lifecycle assignment, replacement identity and ownership policy, attempt receipt, retry and save/migration mapping remain explicit technical/content gates.

## 9. Simulation, mana, offline and durable state — D150, D189–D201 and existing owner locks

- One writable canonical wallet; derive protected required reserve from configured obligations. Research, optional new construction, item minting, traps and acquisitions cannot invent a second wallet. Authorized monster recovery spends protected mana per existing design; new trap/loot spends must be explicitly classified against reserve policy before implementation. A later refund is an **exceptional recovery of previously paid mana**, not world-value income. Never double-credit, double-debit or reprice original investment by current catalog value.
- Simulation and rewards advance on canonical event boundaries independent of rendering, selected floor or frame delta. Multiple parties on distinct floors, per-floor occupancy, downward waiting and exact offline catch-up remain later Living Dungeon implementation dependencies beyond #245's one-floor scope. Vacant-floor restoration and loot restock must honor **actual canonical floor occupancy** and the causally ordered event timeline.
- **D150**: full valid offline absence receives all individually eligible authentic objective/achievement/loot/encounter progress through **exact bounded resumable event simulation or proven mathematically identical optimization**. No arbitrary time cap, invented kill count or outcome-extrapolating shortcut. Preserve existing offline-specific Heat tier restrictions and passive-mana single-grant policy; **do not assume an offline interval uses the same Heat semantics as online**. The research MVP's local verification mode is distinct from public-release backend verification.
- Durable state needs versioned profile/rule compatibility and stable IDs, immutable run/visit captures, single deterministic tie ordering, no duplicate session/settlement/research/stock/trap/achievement receipts, crash-safe exact-once transaction publication and reviewed migrations for any newly persisted fields. Preserve historical executable B1 checkpoint formats and current schema 15 without inferring a new schema number. Invalid/missing/contradictory state fails closed, is diagnosed and does not silently delete paid player assets.
- **Workloads:** bound geometry, status effects, loot candidates, replay, concurrent party records and catch-up batches; target **30 FPS** on supported low-end devices per Spec 36 with real profiling. Do not change authoritative outcomes to rescue a slow phone. #245's sample is not a five-floor/mobile qualification.

## 10. Player agency, visibility, localization and acceptance

- Preserve the **build → run → inspect → revise → rerun** loop. Show *why*: live party actions and actual HP/effects, room layout/LOS and water, mana and affordability, selected loot node stock/empty/waiting, Ward bypass/denial, boss ability recipients and nonstacking, accurate research prerequisite progress and Floor 5 opportunity. Use recorded authoritative evidence rather than presentation-side resimulation.
- Follow Phase 7A0 and the approved PR #242 responsive refinement: portrait-first map with bottom contextual surface; active landscape construction/content context may be on the **right**; floor selector is navigation only; Edit draft is not runtime authority and Save Changes is the durable commit. Avoid inventing broad new inspector frameworks or changing established UI layout without specific UAT evidence.
- All player-facing strings use stable localization keys and existing content ownership. No color-only communication: halo plus distinguishable glyph/text/inspection, accessible scaling, long text/scroll, touch targets and safe areas. Windows Large-text evidence does not qualify native mobile touch or all languages. Do not hide performance, achievement or economy failures behind animation.
- Phase 8 content acceptance requires independent EditMode and PlayMode correctness, versioned save/migration/reopen, deterministic replay and exact offline differential, valid Windows standalone and actual supported-device workload testing, and owner playtests where a player can **explain tradeoffs and voluntarily change layout/composition**. An automatically passing test suite does not prove player experience.

# MVP Gameplay-First Sequencing Decision

**Decision date:** 2026-11-19  
**Verified base:** `main` at `5ab46461fe1bec40108f58e9944ddb17e8bbe52d` (merged PR #268).  
**Status:** Records owner-approved priorities and requirements; this document does not implement or qualify them.  
**Owner sources:** [PR #269 closure decision](https://github.com/gdg3417/Dungeon-Lord/pull/269#issuecomment-7015127781); owner direction captured for this documentation reconciliation.  
**Scope:** MVP development sequence, production UI maturity, room placement/connection capability, Research priority, and onboarding deferral.

## 1. Current state and authority

PR #268, **Build the first room from Dungeon**, is merged. PR #269, **Guide the first Dungeon session**, was closed without merging after owner UAT and repeated independent review. Its closure records a sequencing decision to defer the complete interactive tutorial until the remaining MVP gameplay and production UI are sufficiently mature; it does not abandon onboarding or invalidate the preserved qualification and design evidence. No implementation from #269 is approved by this record. The GitHub history, branch, worktrees, screenshots, tests, reports, protected saves, and qualification evidence remain historical owner artifacts and are not cleanup targets of this change.

Current production Dungeon UI supports actual gameplay and is functionally useful, but is not accepted as the final experience. Owner-reported redesign needs include excessive blockiness, panels that look disconnected, too many prominent buttons, a technical presentation, and an outdated visual style. These are future usability and visual acceptance requirements, not a prerequisite that blocks Phase 8 gameplay. The existing mobile UI vision is a directional reference, not a pixel-perfect specification or visual approval.

Current room construction is narrower than the requested capability. `StructuralEditService` enumerates candidate grid anchors and validates each through authored geometry and route rules. Production construction presents those valid anchors and authored room orientations/connection points. Current room connection resolution uses authored sockets and supports the configured direct doorway or straight-corridor construction path; connection resolution must be unique. `StructuralRenovationService` resolves existing route boundaries deterministically from authored geometry during applicable room renovation. `room_connection_points.csv` defines the current authored room sockets. These implementation facts do not mean that every legal footprint is presently selectable or that players can independently author the requested room-to-room route.

The editor's transactional draft can represent temporary invalid structural edits, with validation and a separate atomic canonical Save Changes operation. That behavior is not permission for a disconnected room to become canonical on an active floor. Required-route validity, stable identities, authored geometry, deterministic resolution, versioned-save compatibility, and existing recovery rules remain governed by approved specifications and runtime authorities.

The approved Phase 8 Research direction is not complete in the current single-slot prototype. The future MVP model has independent General and Blueprint slots, player-initiated starts, configured upfront usable-mana payment, eligible elapsed and offline progress, durable ready/claim/completed state, and exactly-once claim semantics. These are future acceptance requirements, not implementation claims. Existing Research unlock conditions and progression authority remain governed by their specifications and approved Phase 8 decisions.

## 2. Owner-approved priorities and requirements

1. Complete the core playable gameplay mechanics before undertaking another major UI/UX design phase.
2. Continue Phase 8 with functional, temporary production UI that supports real player interaction and gameplay verification. A provisional interface must remain understandable, localization-backed, accessible, and usable for its capability.
3. Treat room placement and player-controlled room connections as unfinished core construction gameplay. The intended placement capability allows a player to position a room on any legally available, sufficient grid-aligned floor footprint, including an offset from the preceding room, rather than limiting placement to one or two aligned connection locations. Room position and its eventual legal connection into the dungeon are distinct player decisions.
4. Preserve the approved direction for player control of route connections, including corridor creation, path suggestion, and segment/bend editing. More selectable anchors alone do not satisfy this requirement.
5. Schedule the construction capability after the initial Phase 8 Research vertical slice and before broad expansion of floors and content systems. Reassess dependencies from the then-current main branch. The work may be divided into bounded draft-positioning and playable corridor/route-authoring PRs if a fresh technical review supports that split.
6. Defer substantial visual modernization toward late Phase 8 or the beginning of Phase 9. Complete visual acceptance before release qualification. Use family/friend gameplay feedback to guide later UI/UX refinement. Small early playtests may still inform mechanics and usability; broad visual approval is not a gate for the next Research PR.
7. Defer the complete interactive onboarding tutorial until its associated mechanics and production UI are sufficiently mature. Preserve the existing soft-guided, skippable approach and recommended-next-steps decisions, plus the future direction recorded in the #269 closure: living-dungeon-core introduction and a small contextual guide sprite; first-room construction; nonexclusive Goblinoid/Goblin or Undead/Skeleton starter choice; actual monster/loot placement; owned-content moving, unassigning, and redeployment; scheduler-controlled visits without fabricated runs; an authoritative compact HUD-style quest tracker separate from tutorial controls; no tutorial quest rewards; Research guidance only when its mechanics/UI exist; dungeon-first navigation and emergent identity. These remain future requirements, not implemented or finally designed mechanics.

## 3. Deferred technical decisions and protected invariants

This record does not select room/socket geometry, rotation behavior beyond existing authored rules, corridor segment representation, pathfinding or routing algorithms, topology permissions, construction or corridor costs, content IDs, data/save schemas, migration behavior, or a new authority. It does not approve unrestricted topology, free-angle rotation, or canonical disconnected rooms. A future design and implementation review must derive these details from the latest repository, approved specs, authored content, and persistence architecture.

Until such review, retain transactional draft semantics, representable temporary invalid states, required-route validation, final atomic commit, deterministic connection resolution, authored geometry, stable identities, save compatibility, fail-closed recovery, localization, accessibility, and all applicable simulation invariants. Room construction work does not have to wait for or bundle with the full visual redesign.

The five-floor main-dungeon MVP remains in force. All sub-dungeons, including Mana Farm, remain excluded from the five-floor MVP and initial public release. This documentation does not restart paused H2 persistence optimization or change already implemented two-floor foreground gameplay.

## 4. Recommended sequence from the verified baseline

After this documentation PR is merged, review the latest `main`, outstanding PRs, save/domain authorities, relevant specifications, and qualification requirements, then make a bounded Phase 8 Research implementation the recommended next gameplay PR. Keep its UI functional enough for players to start and inspect Research and for the slice to be verified. A separate prerequisite save/domain PR is appropriate only if current architecture and migration risk demonstrate the need; infrastructure work must retain a direct player-visible next step.

After the initial Research vertical slice, schedule the reviewed room-positioning and player-controlled connection capability before broad dungeon/floor/content expansion. Continue Phase 8 through dependency-correct workstreams: Research and progression authority; Monsterology and starter-family progression; Architecture, room placement and floor progression; Blueprint and loot economy; Trapcraft, environmental content and advanced combat; and exact offline Living Dungeon processing. These are planning categories, not a fixed PR count or rigid total order. Preserve existing two-floor foreground play and do not restart paused H2 optimization.

Phase 9 remains the validation and hardening stage. Its gate includes functional and visual player-experience acceptance, accessibility, mobile qualification, save integrity, and evidence that players understand the gameplay. Final visual acceptance remains required before release qualification.

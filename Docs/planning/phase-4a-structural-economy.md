## Canonical passive online mana

`Assets/_Project/Resources/passive_online_mana.json` is the production tuning authority for passive online earning. Its validated snapshot owns the temporary `MvpBaselineCoreLevel` value of 1, mana per Core Level per minute, mana per active floor per minute, the complete Peace/Notice/Concern heat-efficiency map, and soft-cap activation/parameters. `MvpBaselineCoreLevel` is an explicitly temporary Phase 4 input, not durable Core Level progression: it is neither persisted nor represented as a second progression authority. Enabled soft caps require authored start and slope parameters. The current approved configuration disables the soft cap and therefore carries no sentinel or invented parameters.

The same validated configuration snapshot now owns `BaseOfflineEfficiency = 0.15`. Offline calculation consumes the canonical applicable online mana/hour result, applies the effective offline efficiency, multiplies by exact elapsed seconds over one hour, and then clamps the single `StructureRuntimeState.ManaReserve` wallet to structural-economy capacity. The configured value is the base layer; the calculation accepts an effective-efficiency input so a later approved Research authority can modify it without replacing lifecycle or persistence. No Research content or persisted speculative modifier is introduced.

Every valid forward interval is eligible, including intervals longer than the existing `maxOfflineSeconds = 86400` diagnostic scaffold. That value and clock-skew warnings do not cap or deny mana. Invalid/missing timestamps and backward movement award zero. Fractional mana is retained. Heat is read from the durable canonical state through the online resolver and is not advanced offline.

Cold start resolves the interval before the boot save can advance `lastSavedUtcUnix`. Pause first establishes a durable boundary; resume resolves once while active ticking remains paused. A detached candidate changes only the wallet and consumed timestamp, validates and reopens the complete schema-9 save, verifies session/disk freshness, persists atomically with durable readback, and publishes runtime only after success. Persistence failure retains the prior live/durable wallet and timestamp and keeps resume paused for a safe retry.

`OfflinePassiveManaResult` is deterministic structured evidence, not a second authority. It exposes saved/observed timestamps, elapsed seconds, canonical online rate, configured base and effective efficiency, pre-cap and actual awards, wallet before/after, capacity, clamp state, and a stable result reason. This is the required client observability seam for pre-MVP anti-cheat monitoring; it does not prove deliberate local-clock manipulation or make stored diagnostics trustworthy evidence.

The pure resolver derives active-floor count from the validated canonical spatial state and the heat band from the existing canonical `StructureRuntimeState.Heat` authority. It resolves mana per hour in the invariant order Base → Heat → Research → Event/Season → Clamp/SoftCap → Rounding. Research and Event/Season are neutral until their canonical authorities exist; the authored Conversion Tuning effect is not active. The rounded authoritative hourly rate is converted to the configured simulation tick's fractional award only after formula resolution, preserving 0.5 mana per 10-second Peace tick and deterministic accumulation without per-tick integer rounding.

GameRoot applies exactly one award on each eligible active TimeService tick and atomically publishes one updated `structureRuntime.ManaReserve`, clamped by the existing `structural_economy.json` ManaCapacity. Paused time produces no tick. Canonical runs do not synthesize an extra passive tick. StructureSimulation retains its legacy compatibility data, but canonical mode suppresses positive legacy `ManaDeltaPerTick` so a Mana Generator cannot become a second production authority. `content_bootstrap.json` owns the existing 10-second tick and the active-save interval; periodic active saving uses the existing SaveService atomic path without saving every tick.

## Save and investment authority

`StructureRuntimeState.ManaReserve` remains the only writable balance. Schema 9 appends `structuralInvestment` after the existing canonical authority, floors and lifecycle/ownership members in the complete primary save. The spatial schema and its fingerprint remain unchanged. The complete-save session owns the ledger; it is not a second wallet or a runtime projection field.

The ledger has exactly one record for each live room and route edge, including doorways with zero initial investment. Fields have strict order: `StructureId`, `ConstructionMana`, `RenovationMana`. Records are ordinal by stable identity. Missing, duplicate, negative, nonfinite, unknown, out-of-order and noncanonical numeric records fail closed. Geometry validation remains independent of investment and wallet state. Ledger records are bounded by the canonical record limit, and the complete payload including the ledger must pass existing serialized node/collection/byte and raw scan/array/byte budgets before persistence.

Construction records the final amount charged. Corridor portions receive their proportional share of the final total rounded down; the room receives the remainder. Thus allocations always sum to the actual paid total even when future flat/percent modifiers and rounding change the total. Movement/replacement adds actual paid renovation mana to the surviving room identity. Existing edge IDs retain investment even when renovation changes between physical corridor and doorway representations.

Retirement follows the existing inverse-tail lifecycle: the old terminal relationship maps to the new incoming relationship on construction, and the removed incoming relationship maps to the new predecessor-to-terminal relationship on deletion. Direct-doorway relationships transfer directly. Physical-corridor ownership follows the ordinal set of occupied tiles shared by the retired and successor relationships with the same corridor definition; it does not follow a fresh edge ID by itself. Historical investment for those carried tiles transfers exactly once, and construction charges only successor tiles outside that carried set. If a successor extends the physical corridor, only its additional tiles receive new actual paid investment. Retired physical tiles retain no successor ownership and their proportional historical investment joins the configured refund basis in the same atomic candidate, including when construction replaces them with different infrastructure. Investment of a removed room or outgoing edge is likewise retired. This keeps active investment equal to the actual eligible payment retained in live infrastructure without catalog repricing or duplicate purchase.

Schemas 1–6 retain their frozen migration into schema 7, then explicitly transition 7 → 8 → 9. Schema 8 upgrades directly to 9 without passing through legacy projection. All old structures receive zero paid investment; unrelated recognized and extension state, balance, identities, counters, assignments, custody and run history are preserved. The schema 8 boundary parser remains explicit. Current schema 9 load is a no-op; migration refuses already-current input. Schema 7/8 starter/contract records remain intact and schema 9 adds its own selection with the same approved geometry.

## Transactions and undo

The write authority refreshes spatial validity and then computes current price/affordability using current live ManaReserve. Displayed economy results are never commit authority. Structural staleness still uses the unchanged spatial fingerprint. Exact balance succeeds. Insufficient funds return `structural.economy.insufficient_mana` before persistence, without publishing geometry, mana, ledger or allocator changes.

Spending/refunding changes only the detached recognized-state snapshot and detached investment ledger. One complete candidate goes through validation, session reopen, exact atomic persistence and durable readback before runtime publication. Failed operations leave runtime unchanged, and existing expected-byte/stale-preview rules prevent duplicate spend/refund on retry.

Refunds floor the configured fraction of removed historical investment. The single economy snapshot owns storage capacity. Newly credited mana is limited to remaining capacity; an existing over-cap balance is preserved and gets no added refund. Spending naturally reduces an over-cap balance. Canonical passive online production and the current unmerged offline passive mana packet use this same capacity authority.

SaveService privately owns one pending renovation inverse in memory. Stopwatch's monotonic timestamp/frequency is the production elapsed-time source, injectable in tests. A successful move/replacement captures the previous spatial state, ledger and exact charged delta. Undo restores those with current unrelated recognized state and adds back exactly the renovation charge, without the deletion refund percentage or capacity clamp. A failed undo persistence retains the pending inverse until expiry. At the configured deadline undo is unavailable. Successful canonical edits (including content assignment, which could otherwise be lost by restoring a layout snapshot), starting a run, service replacement/reopen, or successful undo invalidate the previous inverse. Another renovation can establish a new inverse. There is no serialized undo field and no multi-step stack.

# H2 foreground eligibility amendment

Owner approved 2026-11-15. This amends D3, H1-C02-A and L1 for future H2 sessions, including the H1 recovery handoff in those sessions. D1/D2, D4–D6, the canonical timestamp order and the pre-O1 passive-only crash rule govern. The shipping H1 path continues under its existing contract.

The scheduler owns durable accepted and closed frontiers `A` and `C`. TimeService owns authentic paired monotonic and UTC observations, eligibility and the lifecycle epoch. `O` is its qualified logical horizon, and `W` is the durable accounted wall endpoint at 100 ns precision. `D` is an observed background edge, `R` recovery entry, `S` the sampled recovery closure boundary and `Q` the observed proof/release boundary. All wall segments are half-open. SaveService alone publishes canonical state through the complete-save protocol.

## Schema 17 continuation contract

The current precise owner encodes W as existing saved seconds plus fractional 100 ns units, context v3 and an optional episode. The independent reader validates this shape and the episode range. The scheduler separately encodes and validates A/C, with the established resolved-state checks. In a closed precise save with no episode, advancing W across a classified exclusion is representable as newer accounted coverage. Reopen starts its next absence from W. Existing readers have no new obligation to interpret or replay the exclusion.

That representability does not grant write authority. Ordinary `CanonicalTimeTransition.Foreground` requires eligible predecessor-bound evidence and binds the candidate scheduler's A to the captured horizon. `Enter` and `Recover` carry only their existing episode semantics. The prospective coverage-only case requires a new narrowly validated transition in the existing canonical path. Before adoption it must match exact predecessor bytes, TimeService owner and epoch, current context, path and filesystem ownership, no pending episode, publication proof and closed unchanged A/C. Validate that the chain starts at predecessor W, ends at a genuine observation, covers only the classified excluded gap, has no unresolved earned prefix or background in the covered span and advances W monotonically. All gameplay, resource, Research, checkpoint, allocator, history and monster effects must match the predecessor. Publish atomically and enforce this comparison at acceptance and semantic readback. A coverage cut cannot supply a passive grant or an ordinary acceptance. The exact writer validation and differential recovery tests are implementation entry obligations for this transition.

Keep all context-v3 inputs in this exact order and byte-identical: run simulation, passive online mana, structural economy, monster recovery, generated manifest, generated catalog, foreground v1, foreground v2, room occupancy, compatibility profiles, content bootstrap, and loot. Preserve historical registrations, frozen readers, migration dispatch and the 590-byte provenance reserve. No context substitution, automatic rehash or legacy rewrite.

Engineering owns the technical contract and proofs. The content/build pipeline owns authored policy and activation provenance, with owner approval of limits and target scope. Composition selects one immutable version for a session and binds it to lifecycle/adoption identity. Rebinding requires quiescence, disposal and proven reopen/drain. The policy stays outside the persisted context only while it controls prospective admission and slicing with identical meaning and replay of every accepted obligation. Compatibility tests must enforce this premise.
